What we produce

Yarn

Manufacturing

Production built for scale and precision

Our facilities run fiber, yarn, fabric, chemical and garment production side by side, so quality and lead times are controlled at every handoff — not just at the final stage.

  • Integrated planning across all five divisions
  • Consistent quality control from raw material to finished good
  • Sample and dispatch tracking built into every order
Innovation

Product development that moves with the market

From new constructions to performance finishes, our teams work directly with brand partners to develop fabrics and garments built for what's next — not just what's proven.

  • Continuous construction and finish development
  • Fast sample-to-approval cycles
  • Technical collaboration with brand design teams
Sustainability

Responsible production, built into the process

Traceability and resource efficiency are part of how each division operates — not an add-on. We're building toward cleaner chemistry, lower waste, and full supply-chain visibility.

  • Traceable inputs from fiber to finished product
  • Ongoing investment in cleaner chemical processes
  • Waste and resource-use tracking across facilities
