08

Form Controls

One look app-wide. Controls are 36–40px tall with a visible border and 14px type; compact contexts use the small control height, never mixed with full-height controls in the same group.

09

Tabs

Navigation between views of one record: plain text on a shared divider, 2px accent underline exactly as wide as the active tab. Inactive tabs stay fully readable — never styled as disabled.
